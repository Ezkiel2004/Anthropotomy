How to play

  1. Tap a germ before it sinks back into its pore. +10 each.
  2. Keep a streak going: ×2 after 5 hits, up to ×4.
  3. Spare the round blue bacteria. Tapping one costs 15 and ends your streak.
  4. You have 60 seconds. Germs speed up as time runs out.

Keyboard: pores map to 789 / 456 / 123. Esc pauses.
