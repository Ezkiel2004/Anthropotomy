What you can do

Four ways to get to know the body

Your teacher sets up the lessons, quizzes and models. You reach all of them from one student account.

Fig. 01

Explore in 3D

Open the 3D anatomy explorer and turn the model any way you like. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, and reset the view when you lose your place. Your teacher’s notes on each body system sit right beside the model.

  • Zoom in and out, or let the model turn on its own.
  • Save an image of the view you’re studying.
  • Practise with your teacher’s structure descriptions. It’s ungraded.

Body systems set up at your school:

Fig. 02

Learn in lessons

Your teacher groups lessons into modules. Each lesson keeps its reading, images, PDFs, videos and 3D models together on one page, so you’re not hunting through folders. Finish it, mark it complete, and the next one is waiting.

Fig. 03

Test yourself

Quizzes mix four kinds of question: identification, multiple choice, true or false, and image hotspots, where you click the right spot on a picture. Your answers save as you go. Refresh the page and you’re back in the same attempt, with your saved answers and the time you have left. Grading happens on the server when you submit.

How it works

Three steps to your first lesson

  1. 01

    Create your account

    Sign up with the student ID your school issued, your section and a password of your own.

  2. 02

    Learn with lessons and the 3D explorer

    Open the modules your teacher has published. Read, watch, and turn the models until it clicks.

  3. 03

    Take assessments and track your progress

    Answer the quizzes your teacher assigns, then check your scores and progress whenever you like.
