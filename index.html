Before you sign up.

Is any of this graded?

Only the quizzes your teacher assigns. Quick practice in the 3D explorer and the demo on this page are never graded.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Anthropotomy runs in your web browser, on a school computer, a laptop or your phone.

Will it work on my phone?

Yes. Lessons, quizzes and the 3D explorer all fit small screens. On a phone, the explorer’s tools move to a bar along the bottom.

What do I need to sign up?

Your student ID, section and grade level, an email address, a contact number and a password you choose.

What if I forget my password?

Ask your teacher. They can reset it for you. There’s no email reset, and the sign-in page shows who to contact.

What if my teacher hasn’t added lessons yet?

Lessons and quizzes appear as soon as your teacher publishes them. Until then, the 3D explorer shows every body system your school has made available.
